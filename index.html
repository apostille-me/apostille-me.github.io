Operational clarity is not legal advice.

Apostille Me is case-workflow software. Requirements vary by document, authority, destination, timing, and professional interpretation.

  • Do not place passports, identity scans, private keys, or sensitive case files in logs.
  • Production use requires encryption, tenant isolation, access controls, retention policy, backups, and auditability.
  • Jurisdiction-specific decisions require qualified professional review.
